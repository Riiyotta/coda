Codapay supports 400+ local and global payment methods across over 80 markets, covering 90% of the world’s preferred ways to pay.

This includes credit and debit cards, e-wallets, carrier billing, bank transfers, convenience store payments, cash at retail, vouchers, QR payments, and Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) all managed through a unified platform.

Yes, Codapay functions as a Merchant of Record (MoR) solution, where Coda handles key operational and regulatory responsibilities, including tax compliance, fraud management, and payment processing.

Alternatively, Codapay can also operate under a Payment Orchestration (PO) model, where Coda simply acts as the payment processor connecting publishers with multiple payment options through a single API, and the publisher remains the legal entity.

As the Merchant of Record, Coda calculates and remits applicable taxes where required, across supported markets, while also managing local payment processing and settlement, helping publishers sell internationally without requiring local entities in each local market.
