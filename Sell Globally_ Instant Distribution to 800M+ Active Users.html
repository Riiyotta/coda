Codashop connects content providers with a global user base across 62 markets through an optimized storefront and secure, locally preferred payment options. Providing and promoting in-game currencies for hits like Mobile Legends: Bang Bang’s Diamonds and Honkai: Star Rail’s Oneiric Shards, and top-ups for services like Netflix and Tinder.

Startselect is built for gamers, connecting players across Europe with leading gaming and entertainment brands through a focused storefront and locally preferred payment methods. It provides instant access to game keys, subscriptions and in-game currencies for platforms such as PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo and Steam, helping players stay in the game.

Designed for global reach, Recharge connects consumers with leading brands and services any time, worldwide, through a trusted storefront and secure, locally preferred payment methods. It brings together a broad prepaid offer, including gift cards, gaming credit, mobile top-ups and prepaid credit cards from brands such as Apple, Google Play, PlayStation, Netflix and Vodafone.

The leading online prepaid destination in France since 2013, Recharge.fr connects consumers with top brands and services through a locally tailored storefront and preferred payment options. It supports everyday use cases with mobile top-ups, prepaid credit cards and gift cards for brands such as Orange, SFR, Free and PCS.

Serving consumers in the Netherlands since 2010, Beltegoed connects a large local customer base with brands and services through its familiar, locally optimised experience. It enables access to mobile top-ups, gift cards and more for brands such as KPN, Vodafone, Odido and Bol.com, supported by trusted payment methods like iDEAL–Wero.

Focused on the Flemish market in Belgium, Herladen connects consumers with global and local brands and services through a straightforward, locally relevant storefront. It provides access to mobile top-ups, gift cards and prepaid credit for brands such as Proximus, Orange Belgium and Bol.com.

Serving German-speaking markets, Guthaben has been connecting consumers in Germany and Austria with local and global brands and services since 2013 through a storefront tailored to local purchasing habits. It offers mobile top-ups, gift cards and prepaid credit for brands such as Telekom, Zalando and PaysafeCard, reflecting strong demand for prepaid and alternative payment options.

A go-to prepaid destination in the UK since 2013, Mobiletopup connects consumers with leading brands and services through a fast, reliable storefront and familiar payment options. It provides access to mobile top-ups, gift cards and prepaid credit cards for brands such as EE, O2, Vodafone, Just Eat, Tesco, Sainsbury’s, Asda, Spotify and Netflix, supporting everyday connectivity, shopping and digital spend.

Coda Consumer Platforms are a group of regionally leading Coda owned & operated digital content shops designed to expand the reach of digital content beyond traditional app stores.

Integrating into our platforms requires minimal technical effort and relies on a single API integration.

If your content is already integrated, you can utilize your existing setup to instantly access the network. For new partners, you simply need to submit certain API endpoints through the Coda Portal for testing and completion.

The platforms comprises over a dozen of locally relevant Coda owned and operated consumer shops across multiple global categories. Key shops include:

Codashop, Startselect, Recharge.com, Beltegoed.nl, Herladen.com , Guthaben.de, Mobiletopup.co.uk, Recharge.fr

By joining the platform, you gain access to localized, end-to-end marketing expertise and execution to maximize visibility and drive conversions through partner marketing, co-funded campaigns, or influencer campaigns.

The platform provides a direct-to-consumer and third-party distribution strategy that allows you to bypass the traditional 30% platform fees charged by primary app stores. Instead of losing nearly a third of your revenue, Coda typically operates on a distribution fee that can cut fees by around -50%, significantly improving your margins.
